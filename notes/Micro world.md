## Micro world

### Isotope : 

- Isotopes are different atoms of an element which have   
  the same number of protons but different number of neutrons. 

### Relative atomic mass : 

- It is the weighted average of the relative isotopic masses of all naturally occurring isotopes of an element based on the 12C \= 12.00 scale. 

### Dative covalent bond : 

- Dative covalent bond is the covalent bond in which the bond pair is derived from the same atom. 

  #### Formation of dative covalent bond using H3NBH3 as an example

- B atom in BH3 has only 6 outermost shell electrons.   
  N atom in NH3 has a lone pair.   
  B atom accepts the lone pair on N atom to form a dative covalent bond. 

### Electronegativity : 

- It is the power of the atom in a molecule to attract bond pairs. 

State the effect on the jet of water if the negatively charged rod is replaced by a positively charged rod.

- The water jet will still be attracted towards the rod.   
  Water molecules will orientate themselves in alignment with the electric field so that they will be attracted. 

### Polar molecule(H2S vs SiH4)

- H2S is a polar molecule but SiH4 is a non-polar molecule.   
  H2S molecules are held by stronger van der Waal’s forces. 

### Explain why … is reactive species ? (BH3)

  which doesn’t obey the octet rule.

### Why graphite is brittle?

- In graphite, each layer is held by weak van der Waal’s force.  
  Hence, each layer of graphite can be peeled off easily. 

### Why NaCl is brittle?

- In NaCl, Na+ and Cl– ions are held by ionic bonds.

- When a force is applied, relative movement of the ions can bring ions of the same charge close to each other and will result in repulsion.

### Why Al is malleable and ductile?

- In Al, Al3+ ions and delocalised electrons are held by metallic bonds. 

- If an external force is applied,  
  the layers of metal cations will slide over one another without breaking of metallic bonds. 

### Why ice can float on water?

- Ice has a lower density than water. 
- The attraction between water molecules is hydrogen bond.
- In ice, H2O molecules have a tetrahedral arrangement, packed in an open structure.  
- In liquid water, H2O molecules have relative motion and this leads to the collapse of the open structure.  
- The molecules become more closely packed.
